Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy covers the website at agentloft.promptanddone.com ("the website"), which lets you sign in with Google and open Agentloft running on your own computers from any browser. Agentloft itself is an open-source desktop app (github.com/agentloft/agentloft) that runs on your computer; what it does there stays there.

What the website stores

  • Your Google account's basic profile, when you sign in with Google: its stable account id, email address, name and profile picture address. We ask Google for nothing else (the openid, email and profile scopes only): no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else.
  • Your sign-in sessions: a hash of each session's secret and when it expires (30 days at most). The secret itself lives only in your browser's cookie.
  • The computers you link: each one's name, operating system, Agentloft version, the address it can be reached at (which it reports itself), when it last checked in, and a key it shares with the website, stored encrypted. The key lets the website give your browser a short-lived, one-time ticket to open that computer.
  • Pending links while you approve one (a few minutes): the computer's name and operating system, a short code to compare, and the requesting address, used to limit abuse.

What it doesn't

  • Your work. What you do in Agentloft (your projects, files, conversations with agents, terminals) never passes through the website. Your browser connects to your computer directly, through the address your computer reported; the website only signs you in and hands out the tickets.
  • Tracking. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts or trackers, and nothing is sold or shared.

Who processes it

  • Google signs you in (Google's privacy policy).
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and its database, and keeps short-lived request logs for operating it (Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • If you make a computer public, its address is a Cloudflare Tunnel: Cloudflare carries the connection between your browser and your computer, encrypted between your browser and Cloudflare.

How long, and how to delete it

  • Sessions end when you sign out or after 30 days. Pending links are deleted once they expire.
  • A linked computer is removed when you unlink it (in Agentloft, or with Remove on the website).
  • Delete your account at any time: sign in at agentloft.promptanddone.com/app and choose Delete account at the bottom of the page. Everything above about you is deleted at once, and your computers stop being reachable through the website.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the history is public in the project's repository. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
